# Lab walkthrough · The HPWL lab

Narration transcript (matches the voice-over). Times match site/data/films/lab.clock.en.json.

## The lab

### Open the lab `w01` (00:00:00.000)

- This is the lab. It opens with the teacher's starter layout: total HPWL 52.

### Pick a net `w02` (00:00:07.871)

- Click a net to highlight its box and extreme pins; the table updates at the same time.

## Change the layout

### Drag a cell `w03` (00:00:14.260)

- Drag a cell directly. Move C to (3, 5) and every net it touches is recomputed.

### Keyboard `w04` (00:00:21.043)

- Or use the keyboard: focus a cell and press the arrow keys. Undo steps back at any time.

### Step-by-step scan `w05` (00:00:28.036)

- The step-by-step scan reads one pin at a time and shows how the min and max update.

### Fixed anchors `w06` (00:00:33.844)

- Switch to fixed-anchor mode: A and D are locked, and the status line says why they will not move.

## From the film

### Try this frame `w07` (00:00:40.558)

- While watching, press “Try this frame” and the lab takes over the exact coordinates on screen.

## Challenges and Python

### Challenges `w08` (00:00:47.341)

- Challenges check the real layout in your lab, so different correct arrangements all pass.

### Python practice `w09` (00:00:53.475)
